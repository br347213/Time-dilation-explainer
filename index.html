4 · But HOW can time itself differ?

Watch one flash of light cross the traveler’s cabin.

This is not a clock. It is just two real events: light leaves the traveler’s hand, then hits the ceiling.

This is the crack in “one universal time.” The same two physical events can have different elapsed times for people moving differently, because everyone must still measure light at the same speed.

7 · The mental flip

The part our everyday intuition gets wrong.

We imagine one invisible river of time carrying everyone forward together. Nature does not work that way.

The intuition we grow up with
One master flow of time for the whole universe
What actually happens
Each person accumulates time along their own path
Same departure. Same reunion. Different amount of life in between.
If you want the physics name

The time a person personally accumulates along their path is called proper time. Different paths through spacetime between the same two meetings can contain different amounts of proper time. The traveler’s turnaround makes the round trip different from Earth’s path, so when they reunite the traveler has accumulated less.
