TIME DILATION · HOW CAN THIS ACTUALLY HAPPEN?
The question

How can two people actually live different amounts of time?

Forget the age difference for a minute. First, watch one flash of light. It shows why two people can disagree about how much time passed.

1 · Two meetings

They leave together. They meet again later.

The question is what can happen in between.

Same departure. Same reunion. The amount of time in between does not have to match.
2 · Here is the reason

Watch the same flash of light from two viewpoints.

No clock trick. Just a flash leaving one place in the ship and reaching another.

Traveler inside the shipship looks still

The ship is not moving relative to the traveler, so the flash goes straight across the cabin.

Person on Earthship is moving

From Earth, the ship slides sideways while the flash is moving. The same flash has farther to travel.

Light speed is the samefor both people
→
Earth sees a longer pathbecause the ship moves
→
More time between the eventsin Earth's measurement

The light does not cause the effect. It exposes the rule. Light's speed refuses to change, so measurements of space and time have to.

3 · Why this affects a person

Your body is made of physical processes too.

If less time elapses for the traveler before reunion, fewer cycles of every physical process occur.

The traveler does not feel slowed down. Their heartbeat, thoughts, movement, and chemistry all stay normal relative to each other.

5 · One obvious objection

Why doesn't the traveler say Earth should be younger?

For the full round trip, the two trips are not equivalent.

Earth person

One continuous trip from departure to reunion.

Traveler
turns around

Leaves, changes direction, and comes back. That makes the complete trip different.

So how can one person actually live less time?

Because motion changes how much elapsed time fits between events. Light shows us that this has to be true. A person's body follows that same elapsed time, so less time means less physical change and less aging before reunion.

No clocks are used as the explanation. The light-flash diagrams show the same two physical events from two viewpoints.
